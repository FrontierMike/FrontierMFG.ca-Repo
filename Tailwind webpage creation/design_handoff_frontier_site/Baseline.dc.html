The jargon, in plain English.

You don't need to be technical to benefit from any of this. Here's what the terms actually mean — and why each one matters for your bottom line.

Term 01

What's a KPI?

A "Key Performance Indicator" is simply a number that tells you whether things are going well — like parts per hour, scrap rate, or on-time shipments.

Why it matters: the right few numbers, tracked honestly, keep everyone pointed at the same goal.

Term 02

What's OEE?

"Overall Equipment Effectiveness" rolls three things — how often a machine runs, how fast, and how many good parts it makes — into one honest score out of 100%.

Why it matters: most plants sit near 60%. Every point you recover is capacity you already paid for.

Term 03

What's Industrial IoT?

The "Industrial Internet of Things" just means factory equipment that's connected and can report on itself — sensors and machines sending data automatically instead of someone writing it on a clipboard.

Why it matters: automatic data is accurate data. No more guessing or manual logs that take up valuable time.

Term 04

What's a live dashboard?

Screens — on the floor, in the office, or on your phone — that show your KPIs updating in real time, instead of a report you get days later.

Why it matters: you can act on a problem while the shift is still running, not after it's over.

Why it matters

What clear data actually changes.

Find lost hours

Downtime is the silent profit killer. When every stoppage is logged automatically, the real cost of small, "normal" interruptions finally becomes visible — and fixable.

End the guesswork

Quoting, scheduling, and hiring decisions get far easier when they're based on what your plant really produces — not on a hopeful estimate.

Prove improvements

Change a process and see the impact on the same dashboard the next shift. No more debating whether something "felt" better.

Win more work

Increasingly, larger customers want suppliers who can show real production data. Being able to is becoming a competitive edge.

Common questions

The questions shops actually ask.

Will this work with my older machines?

Yes. The sensors mount to equipment you already own and measure what's happening from the outside — no PLC upgrade or special controller needed. Manual, semi-automatic, and CNC all work.

Do I need IT people or a network project?

No. The sensors are wireless and run on standard wall power, and the dashboard opens in any browser. No rewiring, no server to maintain, no IT project.

How disruptive is installation?

Minimal. We place the sensors during a normal visit — no production downtime and nothing cut into or wired through your machines.

What can it actually track?

The metrics that show how the floor is really running: output and part counts, run vs. idle time, downtime and stoppages, cycle time, and OEE.

Is my production data private?

Yes. Your data is yours — we don't share it, and you control who on your team can see the dashboard.

What if I'm not technical?

That's exactly who Baseline is for. The dashboard is plain-language and built to read at a glance — no data team and no training course required.
